# ads.txt — Authorized Digital Sellers
#
# Most ad networks (including Google AdSense) require this file at your
# site's root (https://yourdomain.com/ads.txt) before they'll serve ads
# or pay out — it's how they prove to advertisers that your site is an
# authorized seller of its own ad inventory.
#
# This file is a placeholder. Once you've signed up with an ad network,
# they will give you an exact line to paste here, typically looking like:
#
#   google.com, pub-0000000000000000, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0
#
# Do not invent this line yourself — it must come from your network's
# own dashboard and match your actual publisher ID (see AD_CONFIG in
# index.html), or ads will not serve correctly.
