If you serve users in the EU/UK, cookie rules (ePrivacy) generally require a consent banner before setting anything beyond strictly-necessary cookies — today this site only sets one strictly-necessary cookie (below), so a banner isn't required for that alone, but you'll need to add one the moment you enable analytics or an ad network. Have a lawyer confirm what applies to your actual audience.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Some are essential for the site to function; others are used for analytics or advertising. This page lists exactly what this site currently uses — and what it doesn't.

2. Cookies we currently use

NamePurposeTypeDuration
bw_sessionKeeps you logged in between visitsStrictly necessary30 days, or until you log out

That's the only cookie this site sets. It's httpOnly (not readable by page scripts) and SameSite=Lax, and contains only a random session identifier — not your email, password, or browsing activity.

3. Local storage (not a cookie, but similar)

If you're not logged in, your "Saved" items are kept in your browser's localStorage so they persist between visits without an account. This data never leaves your browser and isn't sent to our servers — it's cleared if you clear your browser's site data.

4. Cookies we don't currently use (but you should plan for)

  • Analytics cookies — e.g. Google Analytics, Plausible. Not yet integrated. Update this section and add a consent mechanism if/when you add one.
  • Advertising cookies — e.g. from an ad network like Google AdSense, if you enable the ad zones on this site for real ads. These typically involve third-party cookies used for ad targeting, and almost always require a consent banner for EU/UK visitors.

5. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Since this site currently uses only the one strictly-necessary session cookie, blocking it will simply log you out / prevent staying logged in — nothing else on the site depends on cookies.

6. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page whenever the cookies this site actually uses change — this policy is only useful if it stays accurate, so revisit it every time you add a new script, embed, or third-party integration.
